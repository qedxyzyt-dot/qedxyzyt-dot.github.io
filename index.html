Telecommunications capstone · USP

Real-time speech denoising & virtual microphone.

A collaborative engineering project that benchmarked classical and neural denoisers under a shared harness, then carried the selected signal path toward a Windows virtual-capture prototype.

  • Compared causal STFT, OM-LSA/IMCRA, RNNoise, WebRTC APM and DeepFilterNet3.
  • Used objective measurements and blind listening rather than a single quality metric.
  • Validated block timing and transport integrity, and documented where VM scheduling prevented a defensible end-to-end real-time claim.
